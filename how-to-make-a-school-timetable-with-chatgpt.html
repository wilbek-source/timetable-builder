How to make a school timetable with ChatGPT

A step-by-step guide for primary and lower-secondary schools. You will end up with a weekly timetable for every class and every teacher, with no clashes, as a spreadsheet you can download. It takes about 15 minutes of your time plus 5–8 minutes of calculation.

Why ChatGPT alone is not enough

If you simply ask ChatGPT (or any other AI chatbot) to "make a school timetable", it will write one, but it cannot check it. With 10 classes, 15 teachers, shared rooms and part-time staff, the result almost always has hidden clashes: a teacher in two rooms at once, or a class with too few maths lessons. A timetable is a puzzle that has to be calculated, not written.

School Timetable Builder is a free app for ChatGPT and Claude that solves this. ChatGPT does what it is good at (reading your files and asking you questions), and our timetabling engine does the calculation. Every rule you give is a firm rule: either the timetable meets all of them, or you get a plain explanation of what clashes.

What you need

  • A ChatGPT account. The app also works on the free plan.
  • Whatever you already have about next year: staff list, subject hours per grade, bell times, room list, notes from the principal. Messy spreadsheets and Word files are fine.

Step 1: Add the app to ChatGPT

In ChatGPT, open Plugins and search for "School Timetable". Or go straight to School Timetable Builder in ChatGPT.

Step 2: Click Connect

Click Install plugin and then Connect. No account or login is needed.

Step 3: Drop your files into a new chat

Start a new chat, attach your files and write something like "Help me make next year's timetable for our school". You can write in your own language.

Want to try first without your own data? Download our messy sample school (5 files) and use that.

Step 4: Answer the questions

ChatGPT reads every file, sums up what it found and only asks about what it cannot work out itself, one topic at a time. Typical questions: Which teacher covers this subject? Does a blank cell mean "not taught"? Is a teacher unavailable on certain days?

Before anything is sent, the teachers' names are replaced with codes (T01, T02, …), so our service never sees who your teachers are. ChatGPT then checks your data for free and tells you if, for example, a teacher has more lessons than available time.

Step 5: Say go, and wait 5–8 minutes

When the data is complete, ChatGPT asks if it should start. Answer yes. The engine calculates the whole timetable, usually in 5–8 minutes.

Step 6: Download your timetable

You get a spreadsheet with one sheet per class and one per teacher, with the real names put back in.

Step 7: Save the School data file

You also get a "School data" file with everything that was used. Save it. Next time, or when something changes, attach it in a new chat and say what is different, for example "Mrs Hansen now teaches 2 fewer lessons".

Download both files straight away. Our service keeps nothing for more than 24 hours.

Using Claude instead?

It works the same way. In Claude, open Customize → Connectors, search for "School Timetable Builder" and click Connect. See the Claude guide.

What the engine can and cannot do

It handles 5-day weeks with 1–12 lessons a day, teacher availability (e.g. "not after lesson 4 on Fridays"), workload and part-time, planning time with no gaps, double lessons, daily limits per subject, fixed teachers, shared rooms and classes taught at the same time. It does not handle "if possible" wishes weighed against each other, whole-day events or student-choice courses. Full list.
