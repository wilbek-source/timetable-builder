1. Full-size school: Kildebakke School

Grades 0–9 with four classes per grade: 40 classes, 56 teachers, 19 subjects and 9 kinds of special rooms. It also has fixed preparation time, double lessons, PE with two classes sharing a hall, German/French taught two classes together, class teachers, a teacher who works elsewhere on Fridays and a weekly assembly for grade 7. The timetable takes about 5–8 minutes.

What to expect

  • The assistant reads the interview guide, builds the input files, replaces the teacher names with codes (T01, T02, …) and checks the data (a few seconds).
  • It may ask you to confirm a few assumptions. Reply "Yes, go ahead".
  • You then get one timetable per class and per teacher as files, with the real (fictional) names, plus a School data file.
  • To see an explained failure instead, paste school 2 and add "Actually, nobody teaches German." The check then reports that German has no qualified teacher.
