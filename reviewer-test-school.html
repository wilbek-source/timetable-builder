What to expect

  • The assistant reads the interview guide, builds the input files, replaces the teacher names with codes (T01, T02, …) and checks the data (a few seconds).
  • It may ask you to confirm a few assumptions. Reply "Yes, go ahead".
  • The timetable takes about 2–8 minutes. You then get one timetable per class and per teacher as files, with the real (fictional) names, plus a School data file.
  • To see an explained failure instead, say "Actually, nobody teaches German" before confirming: the check then reports that German in grades 5–6 has no qualified teacher.
